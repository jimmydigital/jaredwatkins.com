From an old joke: you don't just tell someone their cat died. You tell them it's on the roof. Then the fire department can't get it down. Then the vet did everything he could.

A $165 billion AI campus in New Mexico is stuck waiting on an 18 mile gas pipeline the state won't approve. So Oracle sent a force majeure notice. That's the "act of God" clause, and it lets them stop paying rent if the place isn't running by 28.

The fun part is who's left holding the bag. Blue Owl owns the developer, and its own investors have spent all year trying to get their money out. Below them, 20 banks are stuck with $18 billion in loans nobody will buy for more than 90 cents on the dollar. Above them sits OpenAI, which lost more in the first half of 26 than it planned to lose all year.

Nobody's dead yet. But Oracle's got a foot on the ladder (headed to the roof).
