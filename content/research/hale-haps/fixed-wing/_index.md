# Fixed-Wing HAPS (Solar & Fuel-Cell Aircraft)

Fixed-wing High Altitude Platform Systems (HAPS) are large, extremely lightweight, uncrewed aircraft designed to loiter in the stratosphere (roughly 60,000-70,000 ft) for weeks to months at a time, most flying on solar power stored overnight in batteries; a smaller group uses hydrogen fuel cells instead. Positioned as a middle layer between conventional UAVs and satellites, they're pitched for persistent telecommunications relay (direct-to-handset coverage, disaster-recovery connectivity), Earth observation, and defense ISR — at lower cost and higher resolution than a satellite, and with far greater endurance than a battery-powered drone.

## Entries

- [AALTO HAPS]({{< relref "aalto-haps.md" >}}) — Airbus subsidiary (Farnborough, UK) operating Zephyr; 67-day continuous-flight world record (Apr 2025); $100M Japanese-consortium investment led by NTT DOCOMO/Space Compass
- [Prismatic Ltd]({{< relref "prismatic.md" >}}) — BAE Systems subsidiary (Alton, UK) building PHASA-35; 24-hour flight above 66,000 ft (Dec 2024); targeting limited operational service from 2026
- [Mira Aerospace]({{< relref "mira-aerospace.md" >}}) — Space42 subsidiary (Abu Dhabi, UAE), formerly a UAVOS–Bayanat JV; ApusNeo18; opened MENA's first HAPS manufacturing facility (May 2025)
- [Kea Aerospace]({{< relref "kea-aerospace.md" >}}) — Christchurch, NZ; Kea Atmos reached 56,284 ft on an 8h20m flight (Feb 2025), the first Southern Hemisphere solar stratospheric flight
- [Swift Engineering]({{< relref "swift-engineering.md" >}}) — San Clemente, CA; Matsushita International Corp-owned diversified aerospace firm; SULE reached 67,000 ft on a 24-hour flight (Jul 2025)
- [NewSpace Research & Technologies]({{< relref "newspace-research-technologies.md" >}}) — Bengaluru, India; iDEX-backed solar HAPS for the Indian Navy; national endurance record of 27 hours at 26,000 ft (May 2024)
- [Stratospheric Platforms Ltd / World Mobile Stratospheric]({{< relref "stratospheric-platforms.md" >}}) — UK-founded hydrogen-powered HAPS for direct-to-handset 5G; acquired Aug 2025 by World Mobile + Protelindo; Islander antenna flight tests mid-2026

**Documented elsewhere on this site:** [AeroVironment]({{< relref "../../robotics/aerial-drones/aeroviroment.md" >}}) operates **Sunglider** (formerly Horus), a solar-electric HAPS developed through its HAPSMobile joint venture with SoftBank — 80m wingspan, ~65,000 ft operating altitude, flight-tested through October 2024. Full entry lives in the Robotics/Aerial Drones section since AeroVironment's core business is broader tactical UAS and loitering munitions.
