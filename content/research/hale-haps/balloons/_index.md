# Stratospheric Balloons

Balloon-based HAPS trade the persistent, indefinite station-keeping of a solar-electric aircraft or airship for lower cost, faster deployment, and (for the maneuverable/directable designs) days-to-weeks of useful dwell time rather than months. This subsection covers the civil/commercial Earth-observation and telecom side of the balloon-HAPS market. Several of the same companies — and some additional balloon vendors whose primary pitch is drone/missile detection rather than imaging or connectivity — are documented in the [Drone Detection]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/_index.md" >}}) section instead; see the cross-references below.

## Entries

- [Near Space Labs]({{< relref "near-space-labs.md" >}}) — Brooklyn, NY; Swift zero-emission balloon robots capture 7cm-resolution imagery for P&C insurance; $20M Series B (Apr 2025) led by Bold Capital Partners

**Documented in [Drone Detection → High-Altitude Platforms]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/_index.md" >}}):** these balloon and aerostat companies have full entries there because their current or emerging mission set centers on detection/ELINT rather than civil imaging or comms —

- [World View / Ondas Stratollite]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/world-view-stratollite.md" >}}) — Tucson stratospheric balloon (EO/IR/SIGINT); acquired by Ondas (ONDS) in 2026 and integrated with Sentrycs counter-UAS and Palantir AIP
- [Aerostar]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/aerostar.md" >}}) — Sioux Falls-based stratospheric balloon and airship maker (Thunderhead); Airbus U.S. partnership; Army HELIOS/HAP-DS experimentation
- [Urban Sky]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/urban-sky.md" >}}) — Denver Microballoon (mHAB); AFRL STRATFI-funded; paired with Applied Intuition's target-recognition sensor for Project Wallabee
- [TCOM]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/tcom.md" >}}) — Maryland tethered aerostat maker; radar/SIGINT payloads; direct descendant of the Army's JLENS program
- [Hemeria / Safran BalMan]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/hemeria-balman.md" >}}) — French maneuverable stratospheric balloon with Safran.AI-enabled real-time ELINT/EW detection
- [Kalam Labs]({{< relref "../../drone-detection/high-altitude-platforms/kalam-labs.md" >}}) — India (Lucknow) balloon-launched stratospheric UAV; EO/IR/hyperspectral payloads deployed along the Line of Actual Control
