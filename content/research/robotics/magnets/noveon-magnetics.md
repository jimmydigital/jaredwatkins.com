## Summary

Noveon Magnetics, Inc. (formerly Urban Mining Company) is a privately held San Marcos, Texas manufacturer of sintered neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) permanent magnets, marketed as EcoFlux magnets and made partly from end-of-life and scrap magnet feedstock. The company says it is the only operational US producer of sintered NdFeB magnets and handles alloy development, coating, magnetization, assembly and inspection at a single 132,000 sq. ft. San Marcos plant. It announced a \$215 million Series C led by One Investment Management in January 2026, and has announced supply agreements with GM, ABB and Nidec Motor plus a Korean joint factory MOU. NdFeB magnets are the binding input for every BLDC and servo motor, which makes Noveon the most important US name on the [Actuators]({{< relref "/research/robotics/actuators/_index.md" >}}) supply-chain "magnet chokepoint" list.

## Key Facts

- **HQ:** 1550 Clovis Barker Road, San Marcos, Texas (third-party directory)
- **Founded:** 2014 (as Urban Mining Company, per CB Insights); commercial site construction began 2018, magnet production by 2020
- **Ownership:** Private; 215 million dollar Series C closed January 2026, led by a \$200M investment from One Investment Management
- **Facility:** 132,000 sq. ft. San Marcos manufacturing center (company); earlier press describes a larger HQ campus on 10 acres
- **Compliance:** Company states products comply with 10 U.S.C. 2533c and DFARS 252.225-7052 specialty-metals rules
- **Customers and offtake (company announcements):** General Motors multi-year agreement (August 2025); ABB long-term supply agreement (August 2025); Nidec Motor 5-year offtake of potentially more than 1,000 tons
- **Expansion:** MOU with Kangwon Energy for a 2,000-ton annual sintered NdFeB factory in South Korea, production expected 2027

## Products and Capabilities

- EcoFlux sintered NdFeB magnets and magnetic assemblies
- Recycling of end-of-life and scrap magnets as feedstock (process licensed from University of Birmingham research per company page)
- Alloy purchases include high-purity NdFeB alloy from Australian Strategic Materials
