## Summary

Vulcan Elements makes rare earth magnets in North Carolina and frames its mission around national security and rebuilding a domestic magnet supply chain for the US and allies. Its website says it already produces high-performance magnets at a Research Triangle Park plant and is building what it calls the largest rare earth magnet facility in the Western World, a 1.1 million square foot site in Benson, NC. Target markets include defense, space, drones, semiconductor fabrication, AI infrastructure, industrial motors, medical technologies and robotics, and the site explicitly ties robotics to compact, high-torque motors and actuators in joints and servo systems. Funding, customers and capacity were not stated on the page reviewed.
