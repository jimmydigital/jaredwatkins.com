## Summary

USA Rare Earth describes an integrated mine-to-magnet supply chain spanning rare earth elements, oxides, metals, alloys, strip-cast material and permanent magnets. Its website lists mining in Brazil and Texas, processing and separation in Brazil, Texas and Colorado, metal, alloy and strip casting in Cheshire, UK (with Lacq, France and Oklahoma planned), and magnet manufacturing in Oklahoma (South Carolina planned). It reports a Commerce Department letter of intent for \$1.6 billion under the CHIPS and Science Act, a \$565 million US DFC financing package for Serra Verde's Brazil operations, and a completed combination with Serra Verde Group on September 4, 2026, plus a CEO transition on October 1, 2026. Robotics is listed among the industries its metals, alloys and magnets are meant to serve, with no further detail; the Stillwater, Oklahoma plant is not named on the homepage reviewed.
