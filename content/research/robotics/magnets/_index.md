## Overview

Permanent magnets are the supply-chain chokepoint behind every BLDC and servo motor in a robot joint (see the NdFeB notes in the [Actuators]({{< relref "/research/robotics/actuators/_index.md" >}}) overview). This section profiles US-located magnet producers and developers that appear on the [OpenTree]({{< relref "/research/robotics/actuators/opentree.md" >}}) map, ranging from an operating sintered NdFeB producer to ramping rare earth projects and a rare-earth-free iron nitride pilot. Capacity and origin claims are company-sourced.
