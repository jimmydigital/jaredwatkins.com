## Summary

ASCO Sintering Co. is a Commerce, California contract manufacturer of complex powder-metallurgy parts. Directory and industry profiles say it began powder-metal operations in 1964, was incorporated in 1971, is employee-owned and ISO 9001 certified, and has won more than 35 Metal Powder Industries Federation design awards. It produces miniature multi-level parts from iron, stainless steel, brass, bronze, aluminum, monel and other powders, including sintered planetary gear carriers and net-shape powder-metal gears processed toward AGMA 8 precision. Listed industries include medical, aerospace and defense and automotive; robotics is not named in the sources reviewed, but powder-metal gears and gear carriers are relevant to compact planetary joint designs. The company website was not fetched in this review.

## Key Facts

- **Address:** 2750 Garfield Avenue, Commerce, California

## Products and Capabilities

- Self-lubricating and multi-level structural parts and assemblies
