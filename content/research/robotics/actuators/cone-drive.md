## Summary

Cone Drive is a Traverse City, Michigan gearbox maker whose website says its products are manufactured in Traverse City and that its gearbox history spans nearly 100 years. The site presents it as part of Timken Precision Drives, alongside SPINEA, CGI and Timken Encoders. Its range covers double-enveloping worm gearing, harmonic gearing (cup, hat and ring series), cycloidal gearing (TwinSpin, DriveSpin, MoveSpin), planetary gearing, slewing drives and Timken magnetic encoders. It explicitly lists humanoid robotics and robotics and automation among its industries, and offers harmonic gearing as a low-to-zero-backlash solution — a US-based alternative in the strain-wave gearbox segment otherwise led by [Harmonic Drive]({{< relref "harmonic-drive.md" >}}).

## Products and Capabilities

- Worm gearing: servo and NEMA worm gearboxes (double enveloping), DuoDrive pinch roll gearboxes
- Harmonic gearing: cup, hat and ring series plus actuator solutions
- Cycloidal gearing: TwinSpin, DriveSpin, MoveSpin
- Planetary gearing; slewing drives and bearings; solar and tracker gearboxes
- Timken modular and absolute magnetic encoders
- Gearbox inspection, reverse engineering, repair and rebuild service center
- Online configurator and quote form
