## Summary

OpenTree is a website that lists non-Chinese parts for robots and drones, with the tagline "Parts you need; risk you don't." Each listed part is shown against the part it replaces. The site is operated by [Sentinel Automata]({{< relref "sentinel-automata.md" >}}), which also lists OpenTree as an affiliated page on LinkedIn, and it states it is not affiliated with Unitree or the G1 trademark owners. It is aimed at robotics companies facing the FCC's new robot rule and its Buy American–style domestic-content definitions, and it doubles as a sourcing map for the US component supply chain described in the [Actuators overview]({{< relref "_index.md" >}}).

## Key Facts

- **Operator:** Sentinel Automata (founder Ian Daily); pre-production, with a Long Beach, CA pin on its own map
- **Business model:** Not stated — the site has a parts catalog, a "List parts" page for suppliers and a "Reserve" page, but does not say whether it sells directly or takes commissions
- **Supplier listing:** Non-China makers can list by December 31, 2026; listing is free through 2027 with no contract
- **Tools and content:** parts catalog; "Our joints" page; guides; map; FCC 65% calculator that totals a robot's US-made component cost in the browser
- **Site statistics (company-reported):** a typical robot scores 27% US parts by cost, or 73% after swapping in US joints; two joints listed; no drone parts yet
- **Contact:** hello@opentree.shop

## The US Component Map

The map, last checked November 6, 2026, shows 40 pinned companies at 52 locations, and says 60 more companies are listed below the pins across 20 states. Inclusion rules stated on the page:

- A pin requires the company's own site to say it makes the part at that location; offices and foreign makers' US sales arms are listed but not pinned.
- Makers controlled by a PRC-controlled company, and companies on the 1260H or Entity lists, are excluded.
- Origin and location claims come from company websites and are unverified; "not stated" means not found.
- The map presents listing facts, not endorsements or legal certification.

## Why It Matters

OpenTree is both a sourcing tool and a market-positioning move for its operator: it gives US robotics buyers a way to check bill-of-materials provenance while directing attention to US-made joints, which is Sentinel Automata's own product category. Readers should weigh it as a company-run directory rather than a neutral registry.
