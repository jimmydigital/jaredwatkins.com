## Summary

Harmonic Drive Systems (TSE: 6324) makes strain wave gearing, a compact zero-backlash reducer used in industrial robot joints, semiconductor equipment, medical devices, machine tools and satellites. Founded in 1970 through a merger of Hasegawa Gear Works and USM Co., it has consolidated sales, manufacturing and development at the Hotaka Plant in Azumino, Nagano. FY2026/3 (ended 2026-03-31) net sales were a record ¥59.6B (+7.0%) with orders up 16.2% to ¥61.6B. In August 2026 it raised its FY2027/3 forecast to ¥74.5B of net sales on a first-quarter result of ¥16.7B (+23.6%), citing automation investment, surgical support robot orders and semiconductor equipment demand. ## What It Is / How It Works

A strain wave gear has a wave generator (elliptical bearing), a flexible cup-shaped flexspline and a rigid circular spline. As the wave generator rotates it deforms the flexspline so teeth engage at two opposite points, producing a large reduction in a small package with essentially no backlash. That combination is why it is used in robot wrists and small joints, surgical systems and satellites.
