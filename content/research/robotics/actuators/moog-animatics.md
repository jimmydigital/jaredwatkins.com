## Summary

Moog Animatics is a Moog Inc. business whose core product is the SmartMotor, a fully integrated brushless servo motor rated up to 1 kW that combines motor, drive and controller. Variants include a semiconductor version for ISO 4 / Class 10 cleanrooms, an AGV version operating at 48 V or lower and sealed to IP65/67, and a cold-environment version rated for startup down to -46°C. Supported protocols shown include CANopen, EtherCAT, EtherNet/IP, PROFINET and Modbus. The homepage reviewed does not mention robotics by name or state a headquarters or manufacturing location; the OpenTree map pins Murphy, North Carolina.
