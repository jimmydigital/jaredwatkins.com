## Summary

Nabtesco (TSE: 6268) was established in 2003 through the integration of Teijin Seiki and NABCO and reports three segments: Component Solutions (which includes precision reduction gears), Transport Solutions and Accessibility Solutions. Its RV reducer is a cycloidal gear used in the heavier joints of industrial robot arms; the company's own estimate is about 60% of the global market for reducers in the joints of medium-to-large industrial robots. FY2025 (ended December 2025) net sales were ¥307.9B. For the first half of FY2026 (IFRS) it reported net sales of ¥167.4B, operating profit of ¥15.8B (+72.4%) and net income of ¥12.4B (+90.6%), and on 2026-07-31 raised its full-year forecast to ¥344.0B of sales. ## What It Is / How It Works

The RV (Rotate Vector) reducer uses an eccentric drive and cycloidal discs to give high torque capacity, rigidity and overload resistance in a compact package. Nabtesco describes it as supporting fast acceleration, smooth motion, low backlash and accurate positioning, and names industrial robots on automobile lines as a main application. It sits in the base, shoulder and elbow joints of industrial arms, complementing strain wave gears from [Harmonic Drive Systems]({{< relref "harmonic-drive.md" >}}) in smaller joints.

The July 2026 summary says precision reduction gears were among the drivers of steady demand, without quantifying them; the company's robot page does not mention humanoid robots.
