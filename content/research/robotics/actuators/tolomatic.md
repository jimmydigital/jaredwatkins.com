## Summary

Tolomatic, Inc. is a Hamel, Minnesota–based manufacturer of linear motion products. Its website describes it as a US manufacturer of linear motion solutions and says it designs, engineers, and manufactures linear actuators in the USA. Its range centers on electric rod and rodless actuators, servo linear actuators with integrated servo motors, and ServoWeld actuators for robotic resistance spot welding. It sits at the linear-actuation end of the robotics actuator supply chain, complementing the rotary joint and gearbox suppliers elsewhere in the [Actuators]({{< relref "_index.md" >}}) section.

## Products and Capabilities

- Electric linear actuators (rod and rodless)
- Servo linear actuators with an integrated servo motor that adapt to the customer's drives and controls
- ServoWeld actuators for robotic resistance spot welding, with servo and stepper control
- Resold or paired motors and drives: JVL integrated servo and stepper motors and Nidec servo systems
- Power transmission: roller screws, right-angle gearboxes, brakes, clutches
- Pneumatic linear actuators: rodless cylinders, linear slides, thrusters
- Company states its electric actuators have force capabilities that equal or surpass hydraulic systems; a press release says the RSX50 doubled the force capacity of its RSX line
- Online sizing tool, drive integration tool and CAD library
