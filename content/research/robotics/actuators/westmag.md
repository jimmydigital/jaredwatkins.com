## Summary

Westmag (Western Magnetics Company) is a South San Francisco startup that says it designs and manufactures drone motors and robot actuators, with a drone motor at the core of every actuator. Its site lists drone motors in 20–100 mm stator sizes for 5–50 inch propellers (Kv 100 to 3,000, outrunner or inrunner) and robot actuators from 1 to 150 Nm at 24–48 V with 6:1–12:1 gearing, aimed at humanoid and mobile robots. It says it is ramping production and testing at "Factory 01" in South San Francisco with automated winding, stator stamping and coating, a "domestic + allied supply chain," and NDAA-compliant status by default. The site names co-founders David Hansen (CEO) and Jordan Sanders (COO) and shows investor logos including Andreessen Horowitz, Founders Fund, Lux Capital, Menlo Ventures and NFDG, without explaining the relationships.
