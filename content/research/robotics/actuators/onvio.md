## Summary

Onvio LLC is a privately held precision motion-component company headquartered in Salem, New Hampshire. Its About page says it has made components for automation, robotics and motion control for over 35 years and lists planetary gearboxes, cycloidal reducers, timing pulleys, wheel motors for mobile robots and custom assemblies. Trade press records that it changed its name from Mectrol Corp. in 2004 and moved into a 45,000 square foot Salem facility in 2007 for its servo reducer line. An important nuance for US-sourcing work: the same About page says its primary manufacturing facility is a wholly owned subsidiary in Puebla, Mexico, so the OpenTree pin at Salem should be read as headquarters and US operations rather than confirmation that all products are made in the United States.
