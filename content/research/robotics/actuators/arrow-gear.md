## Summary

Arrow Gear Company is a Downers Grove, Illinois gear manufacturer founded in 1947 by James J. Cervinka and Frank E. Pielsticker, in Worth, Illinois, before moving to Lyons in the 1950s and Downers Grove in 1961. It specializes in bevel gears and also makes spur and helical gears, curvic couplings, gearboxes and custom gears, with heat treating and superfinishing in-house. Its website emphasizes aircraft-quality, flight-safety-critical gearing for defense and commercial fixed-wing, helicopter, UAV and satellite markets; robotics is not mentioned on the homepage reviewed.

- Bevel gears; parallel-axis (spur and helical) gears; stock gears
- Curvic couplings, gearboxes and gear sub-assemblies, aerospace gearing
