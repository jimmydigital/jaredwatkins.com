## Summary

Gear Motions is a Central New York precision gear manufacturer that says all its gears are made in the USA and cites over 100 years of experience. Its website shows an ESOP (employee-owned) structure and three locations: Buffalo Operations (Oliver and Niagara Gear) in Tonawanda, and two Syracuse divisions (Nixon Gear and AutoGear). It makes precision ground helical, spur, pump, bevel and worm gears plus sprockets, timing pulleys, shafts, splines and gearboxes, and lists aerospace and military and material handling and robotics among its served markets without giving project examples.
