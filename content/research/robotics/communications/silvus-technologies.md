## Summary

Silvus Technologies, headquartered in Los Angeles, makes StreamCaster MANET radios built on its MN-MIMO waveform, used in defense, law enforcement and public safety. Motorola Solutions completed its acquisition on 2025-08-06 for about $4.4B upfront (about $4.38B cash plus about $20M restricted stock) with an earnout of up to $600M tied to performance in consecutive twelve-month periods ending in 2027 and 2028. In January 2026 Silvus announced the StreamCaster Mini 5200, a 182 g radio, and on 2026-05-14 Motorola announced a $100M, 165,000 sq ft Salt Lake City plant expected to add 200 jobs.

## What It Is / How It Works

MANET radios form a mesh in which each node also relays traffic for others. Silvus's MN-MIMO waveform applies multi-antenna techniques across the mesh for throughput and resilience; the company describes focus on high-throughput connectivity, spectrum awareness and electronic warfare resilience. The Mini 5200 extends the line to a dismounted-soldier size and works alone or with StreamCaster NEXUS.

**Under Motorola.** Motorola's CEO described Silvus as complementary to its land mobile radio and video technologies; the Salt Lake City plant is meant to raise StreamCaster output.
