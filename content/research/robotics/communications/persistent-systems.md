## Summary

Persistent Systems, LLC, headquartered at 601 West 26th Street, New York, builds the Wave Relay mobile ad hoc network (MANET) and the MPU5 radio that runs it. The MPU5 is an Android-based radio with 3×3 MIMO and up to 10 W transmit power; the company lists it as IP68-rated and Blue UAS-certified. Its company news feed reports an $87.5M US Army order for MANET devices in the NGC2 prototype (2026-02-13), more than 2,000 MPU5 radios for the Royal Marines (2025-11-05), the 4th Infantry Division equipped with Wave Relay devices for NGC2 (2025-10-27), a US Navy unmanned surface vessel communications selection (2025-06-17) and NSA CSfC approval (2025-06-13). It competes mainly with [Silvus Technologies]({{< relref "silvus-technologies.md" >}}).

## What It Is / How It Works

Wave Relay is a self-forming, self-healing mesh: any node can route traffic for any other through multiple hops without a base station. The MPU5 packages the radio with an Android computer so applications such as ATAK can run on the device; the company's Royal Marines release describes sharing voice, video, text, GPS location and sensor data, including from unmanned systems, directly between devices.

**Ecosystem strategy.** Third-party drones, ground robots and sensors join the Wave Relay Ecosystem to share one network and command-and-control; once a program standardizes on the network, adjacent hardware follows.
