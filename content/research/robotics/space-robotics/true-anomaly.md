## Summary

True Anomaly is a Colorado-based defense technology company, founded in 2022, describing itself as "the only defense technology company focused exclusively on space defense." It builds **Jackal**, an autonomous orbital spacecraft platform marketed as a "tactical fighter-interceptor" for rendezvous-and-proximity operations (RPO) and space-domain-awareness missions, and **Mosaic**, a mission-autonomy software platform intended to translate operator/commander intent into autonomous spacecraft action across contested orbital environments. The company raised a $650 million Series D in April 2026 (surpassing $1 billion in cumulative funding since founding) and is a named prime contractor on the US Space Force's Andromeda program (Geosynchronous Reconnaissance and Surveillance Constellation, reportedly $1.8B) and the Space Force Space Systems Command's Space-Based Interceptor program. **Editorial note on placement:** True Anomaly is not a mechanisms/tools supplier like [Honeybee Robotics]({{< relref "honeybee-robotics.md" >}}); it builds complete autonomous orbital vehicles for a defense mission, structurally closer to how [naval-drones]({{< relref "../naval-drones/_index.md" >}}) and [undersea-drones]({{< relref "../undersea-drones/_index.md" >}}) cover autonomous vehicles for other warfighting domains. It is filed under Space Robotics because that is this site's only current subsection for autonomous space-vehicle companies; if this subsection grows, a dedicated "orbital defense" split may be warranted.

## What It Is / How It Works

True Anomaly's core products are **Jackal**, described as a "multirole space superiority platform" with a modular architecture, high-thrust/high-delta-v propulsion for maneuvering across multiple orbital regimes, and multiple "mission hardpoints" to host sensors and effectors — company language explicitly analogous to a fighter aircraft's weapons stations, reflecting the company's framing of orbital space as a warfighting domain rather than a purely scientific or communications environment. **Mosaic** is the accompanying software layer: a "ground-up" autonomy platform intended to let human operators set mission objectives (maintaining "human command, machine control" per the company's stated design philosophy) while the software handles real-time maneuver, sensing, and response execution. The company also states it uses a single Mosaic interface across training/simulation and live operations, aiming to build operator proficiency through repeated use of the same tools rather than separate training and operational systems.

True Anomaly's stated mission rationale centers on the argument that orbital space has shifted from a "sanctuary" to a contested warfighting domain, citing China's 2007 direct-ascent anti-satellite test, expanding Chinese counterspace capabilities (the company cites over 1,300 Chinese satellites on orbit, 510+ described as ISR-capable, as of its April 2026 Series D announcement), and Russian ASAT demonstrations, GPS/SATCOM jamming, and reported interest in orbital nuclear weapons. The company frames its own strategy as building many lower-cost, faster-fielded systems distributed across orbits rather than a small number of exquisite, expensive satellites — an approach it compares to the shift from small numbers of exquisite Cold War reconnaissance satellites toward distributed, resilient constellations.
