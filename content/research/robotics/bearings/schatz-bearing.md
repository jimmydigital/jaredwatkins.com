## Summary

Schatz Bearing Corporation makes aerospace, space, precision, thin-section, angular-contact, semiconductor, military, medical and custom bearings at a single facility in Poughkeepsie, New York, which its website says is AS9100 Rev D, ISO 9001:2015 and Nadcap certified. The site says products are made in the USA, approved to Boeing and US military specifications, and held to tolerances measured in millionths of an inch, with ring and ball materials including SAE 52100, 440C, CREN and M50 and hybrid ceramic options. It says it has decades of experience with bearings for automation and robotics, without listing robotics products. The homepage also reports that Arxis, Inc. (Nasdaq: ARXS), described as an industrial compounder formed with Arcline Investment Management, completed its acquisition of Schatz on September 2, 2026 — worth noting because it changes the ownership of one of the few US precision bearing makers.
