## Summary

Kaydon is a Muskegon, Michigan–based bearing manufacturer that its website describes as an SKF Group company. It is best known for Reali-Slim and Ultra-Slim thin-section bearings (bores from 3/4 to 40 inches) and slewing ring bearings (outside diameters from 4 to 282 inches), plus bearing remanufacturing and repair. Thin-section bearings are the typical choice for compact robot joints, gimbals and turret mechanisms, and the site lists robotics, aerospace and defense market pages. This is the largest-scale US thin-section bearing supplier on the OpenTree map, though it is owned by a Swedish group.
