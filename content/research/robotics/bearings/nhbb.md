## Summary

New Hampshire Ball Bearings, Inc. (NHBB) is headquartered in Peterborough, New Hampshire and is a subsidiary of MinebeaMitsumi Inc., operating within the MinebeaMitsumi Aerospace brand. It makes miniature and instrument bearings, medium-to-large and cylindrical roller bearings, thin-section bearings, airframe control bearings, rod-end and spherical bearings, and precision mechanical subassemblies, with designs qualified to AS7949. Its website emphasizes aerospace and defense, high technology and medical markets and does not mention robotics. The OpenTree map pins Peterborough, Laconia (NH) and Chatsworth (CA); only Peterborough appears on the homepage reviewed.
