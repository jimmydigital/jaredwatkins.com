## Overview

Bearings carry load and set stiffness and rotational accuracy in robot joints, gimbals and slewing mechanisms; thin-section bearings in particular let designers keep hollow bores for cabling in compact actuators. This section profiles US-located bearing makers that appear on the [OpenTree]({{< relref "/research/robotics/actuators/opentree.md" >}}) component map. Information is company-sourced and the map's own origin claims are unverified.
