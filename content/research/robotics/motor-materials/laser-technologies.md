## Summary

Laser Technologies, Inc. is a Naperville, Illinois contract manufacturer specializing in laser-cut and stamped laminations for the motor and generator industry, plus laser welding, lamination coating and refurbishment, machining, stamping and assembly. Profiles say it was established in 1985, operates from a 168,000 square foot facility with 20 CNC laser cutting machines, and calls itself the largest electrical steel lamination laser cutter in North America. A trade listing credits it with ISO 9001:2015, ITAR and Nadcap accreditation; a 2023 state manufacturing-extension success story lists 147 employees. Robotics is not named in the sources reviewed; its relevance is as a domestic source of rapid-turn stator and rotor laminations for custom and low-volume motor builds. The company website was not fetched.

## Key Facts

- **Address:** 1120 Frontenac Road, Naperville, Illinois
- **Founded:** 1985 (company-sourced profile text); one directory says 1975
- **Size:** 168,000 sq ft facility; 20 CNC laser cutting machines; 147 employees in 2023 (IMEC)
- **Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015, ITAR registered, Nadcap accredited (trade directory listing)
- **Government registration:** CAGE code 1XD76 (federal contractor directory)

## Products and Capabilities

- Laser-cut and stamped motor, stator, rotor and pole laminations; notched laminations
- Lamination coating, annealing, refurbishment; core assemblies; insulators (Nomex, Kapton, G10)
- Laser welding, MIG welding and riveted pole assemblies; CNC machining and contract manufacturing
