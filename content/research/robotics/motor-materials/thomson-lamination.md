## Summary

Thomson Lamination Company (TLC) is a Maple Shade, New Jersey manufacturer of precision motor laminations, founded in 1964 by John B. Thomson Jr. and still at its original industrial-park site. A sponsored Thomasnet profile says it provides prototyping, tool design and fabrication, stamping, annealing and assembly for precision motor laminations used in aerospace, medical, defense and robotics, and has expanded to 76,000 sq ft across six buildings. Directory entries list electric motor subassemblies, rotors, stators and laminations made from electrical steel alloys, including silicon steel, cobalt and nickel alloys and Hiperco 50. The company's own website blocked automated fetching, so this entry relies on search-surfaced profiles; stator and rotor laminations are core inputs to frameless robot-joint motors.

## Key Facts

- **HQ:** 504 East Linwood Avenue, Maple Shade, New Jersey
- **Founded:** 1964, by John B. Thomson Jr.
- **Ownership:** Privately held (Subchapter S corporation per a directory)
- **Footprint:** 76,000 sq ft across six buildings in Maple Shade (sponsored profile)
- **Markets:** Aerospace, medical, defense, robotics (sponsored profile); guidance and control, process control
- **Materials:** Silicon steel, cobalt and nickel alloys, electrical steel, Hiperco 50

## Products and Capabilities

- Precision motor lamination stamping (small to large) and tooling: carbide dies, tool and die design and fabrication
- Annealing and heat treating, assembly and machining, prototyping
- Rotors, stators and electric motor subassemblies
