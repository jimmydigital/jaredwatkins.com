## Summary

MWS Wire Industries describes itself as a trusted magnet wire supplier and custom producer of bare and insulated specialty alloy wire, located at 3000 Camino Del Sol, Oxnard, California, with over 50 years as an industry leader and an Elektrisola company parent. Products include magnet wire (Multifilar, Twistite and Microsquare brands) and specialty wires: sensor, litz, flat, plated, stainless steel, aluminum, clad metal and resistance wire. The site is labeled Made in the USA and links ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016 certificates and an ISO-certified testing lab. Highlighted markets are medical and aerospace wire for commercial aircraft, defense and space, plus automotive, computers, electronics, sensors and telecommunications; robotics is not mentioned.
