## Overview

Frameless and servo motors depend on stacked electrical-steel laminations, insulated winding wire and fabricated rotors and stators upstream of the magnet and bearing layers. This section profiles US-located lamination and wire suppliers on the [OpenTree]({{< relref "/research/robotics/actuators/opentree.md" >}}) map. Several entries rely on directory and trade sources because company sites blocked or lacked detail, and each entry says so.
