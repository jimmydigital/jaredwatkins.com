## Summary

Wingcopter is a German developer of tilt-rotor VTOL fixed-wing drones, founded in 2017 by Tom Plümmer, Jonathan Hesselbarth and Ansgar Kadura (earlier entry; roles not re-verified). Its Wingcopter 198 was built for cargo and humanitarian delivery. In September 2026 it launched the Wingcopter 262, a 10 kg-class tactical drone with up to 6 hours of endurance and 150 km range, and a dedicated Wingcopter Security & Defence (WSD) business unit; TAF Industries, described as Ukraine's largest UAV producer, is to mass-produce it in Germany under "Build with Ukraine" after a 2026 joint venture. The last funding round confirmed in this review is a $42M Series A extension (2022-06-21) with new investors including REWE Group, Salvia, XAI Technologies and ITOCHU.

## What It Is / How It Works

Wingcopter's aircraft tilt their rotors from vertical lift to wing-borne cruise, which extends range compared with a multirotor of similar size and removes the need for a runway, launcher or catapult. The 198 is its delivery platform; the 262 is a larger-wingspan, lower-weight tactical aircraft aimed at reconnaissance, with one operator able to deploy it in minutes per the September 2026 launch coverage.

**Defense pivot.** WSD is described as a permanent shift toward security and defense. The 262 article names Ukrainian forces as the intended user and says TAF Industries will produce it in Germany; it reports no funding, orders or executives, and does not mention the 198.

**Delivery heritage.** The 2022 funding article reports a partnership with Continental Drones to deploy 12,000 Wingcopter drones in Africa; the sources reviewed do not confirm deployment at that scale.

## Supply Chain Position

Wingcopter operates as a **Platform OEM** for commercial delivery drones. The company designs and manufactures the Wingcopter 198 airframe, tilt-rotor mechanism, and flight control system in Germany. It sources lithium battery packs, BLDC motors, and avionics components from external suppliers (not publicly disclosed). **⚑ Rare earth dependency:** BLDC motor magnets depend on Chinese rare earth processing for NdFeB materials. The company sells complete drone systems and drone-as-a-service (DaaS) contracts to operators in healthcare logistics and commercial delivery sectors.
