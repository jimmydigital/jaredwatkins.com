## Summary

Saule Technologies is a Wroclaw, Poland developer of perovskite solar modules that was once seen as a leader in the technology. In 2025 it fell into a dispute between co-founder Olga Malinkiewicz and its investor Columbus Energy, and a May 2025 report said it faced bankruptcy. On 2026-06-19 the Warsaw Stock Exchange decided to exclude its shares from NewConnect, effective 2026-06-26, because the company had not published annual reports for fiscal 2024 and 2025. Malinkiewicz says no new board has been appointed and operations have not resumed; those are her claims.
