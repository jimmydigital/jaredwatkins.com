## Summary

Tandem PV is a US perovskite-silicon tandem developer. It raised a $50M Series A and debt round in March 2025, led by Eclipse, and in April 2026 opened a roughly 40 MW, 65,000 sq ft factory in Fremont, California. The company says its panels reach 29.7% efficiency in internal testing and that initial modules will ship for customer validation later in 2026, with high-volume manufacturing targeted for 2028.

## What It Is / How It Works

A thin perovskite absorber, about 200 times thinner than silicon, is stacked on a conventional silicon cell so the pair captures a wider part of the spectrum. The company designs the product to fit existing silicon module infrastructure.

## Supply Chain Position

Tandem PV adds a perovskite layer to silicon cells made on conventional lines, so it depends on silicon cell supply and on its own perovskite deposition. Its Fremont plant is described as demonstration-scale, not high-volume.
