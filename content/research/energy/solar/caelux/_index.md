## Summary

Caelux is a Baldwin Park, California solar manufacturer that makes perovskite-coated glass, branded Active Glass, which is paired with a silicon solar module to form a "Hybrid Tandem" module. The company completed its first commercial shipment on 2025-07-08 to an unnamed US solar developer, with an unnamed silicon manufacturer finishing the tandem modules. It raised a $12M Series A3 in August 2023, bringing Series A funding to $24M.

## Key Facts

- **HQ:** Baldwin Park, California (datelined on the July 2025 release)
- **Type:** Private
- **Product:** Active Glass, a perovskite coating on glass that the company says is compatible with all mainstream silicon technologies; Caelux One was the product named in the 2023 funding report
- **Funding:** $12M Series A3 (2023-08-30 report) led by Temasek with Reliance New Energy, Khosla Ventures, Mitsui Fudosan and Fine Structure Ventures; $24M total Series A
- **Manufacturing:** a Baldwin Park facility under construction in 2023, ramping toward 100 MW of perovskite-coated glass
- **Commercial status:** first commercial shipment 2025-07-08; quantity, destination and capacity not disclosed
- **CEO:** Scott Graybeal

## What It Is / How It Works

Instead of selling a full tandem cell, Caelux coats glass with a perovskite layer that a silicon manufacturer laminates onto a silicon module. The perovskite layer absorbs part of the spectrum and the silicon beneath absorbs the rest, so the pair produces more power per unit area than the silicon module alone. Caelux says this works with all mainstream silicon technologies, which lets it sell into existing module lines.
