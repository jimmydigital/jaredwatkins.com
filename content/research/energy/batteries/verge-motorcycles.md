## Summary

Verge Motorcycles is a Finnish electric motorcycle company founded in 2018 in Seinäjoki, best known for its hubless in-wheel "Donut" motor. It is the launch vehicle for [Donut Lab]({{< relref "donut-lab.md" >}})'s battery: Donut Lab was spun out of Verge in August 2024 and the two share founders and owners. The TS Pro is on sale in limited volume (about 350 bikes reported for 2026), with order backlog extending into 2027, and whether its pack is truly solid-state remains unverified by independent chemistry analysis.

## What It Is / How It Works

Verge's distinguishing technology predates the new battery: the Donut motor integrates rotor and drivetrain into the rear wheel with no axle through the hub, which Verge says makes the second-generation unit 50% lighter than its predecessor ([New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/motorcycles/verge-revamps-ts-pro/)).

The corporate relationship with Donut Lab is not arm's length. Donut Lab was founded in August 2024 as a spin-off from Verge to promote the Donut motor, and is owned equally by two Finnish holding companies; Verge and Donut Lab raised €15 million together in 2024 before separating, and Donut Lab raised a €25 million seed round in July 2025 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verge_Motorcycles)). The TS Pro therefore functions both as a product and as the first real-world showcase for Donut Lab's cells.

A small pack relative to automotive use makes a high charge rate a meaningful differentiator for a motorcycle. VTT has independently measured fast charging on Donut Lab cells (see Donut Lab entry), but its March 2026 pack test measured cell behaviour rather than the full vehicle pack, and no independent test has established the cell chemistry or cycle life. Electrek reports that the cell VTT tested for energy density in September 2026 is not the one that will ship to customers ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/?p=482309)).

## Supply Chain Position

Verge operates at the **OEM Integration** layer: it integrates battery packs and sells motorcycles directly to consumers. It does not manufacture cells; [Donut Lab]({{< relref "donut-lab.md" >}}) supplies them (Donut Lab names Nordic Nano as its manufacturing partner). No third-party battery supplier is disclosed, so supply is captive to an affiliated company, and any production or validation problem at Donut Lab directly limits Verge's deliveries. Low planned volume (about 350 bikes in 2026) and pending EU/US certification are further constraints.
