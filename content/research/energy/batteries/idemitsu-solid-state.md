## Summary

Idemitsu Kosan is a Japanese petroleum and chemicals company that has researched sulfide solid electrolytes since the mid-1990s and is Toyota's partner in mass-producing them. It is building a lithium sulfide (Li₂S) plant at its Chiba Complex for about ¥21.3 billion, due in June 2027, and began construction of a larger solid electrolyte pilot plant in January 2026. Whether Toyota reaches its 2027–2028 all-solid-state EV target depends in part on Idemitsu's materials scale-up.

## Key Facts

- **Type:** Public Japanese petroleum and chemicals company (pivoting part of its business to battery materials)
- **Solid electrolyte R&D history:** Idemitsu researched all-solid-state battery elemental technologies from 2001; CEO Shunichi Kito says it discovered the usefulness of sulfur components in the mid-1990s; Toyota says Idemitsu has been its partner on the battery cracking issue since 2013 ([Toyota](https://global.toyota/en/newsroom/corporate/39865919.html), [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/02/09/big-oil-is-betting-big-on-toyota-to-win-the-solid-state-battery-race/))
- **Toyota cooperation:** announced 12 October 2023, covering sulfide solid electrolyte mass-production technology and supply chain; the announcement does not mention exclusivity ([Toyota](https://global.toyota/en/newsroom/corporate/39865919.html))
- **Lithium sulfide plant (Chiba Complex):** about ¥21.3 billion with a ¥7.1 billion METI grant; about 1,000 t per year (equivalent to about 3 GWh per year of batteries); operational June 2027 ([Electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2025/03/01/idemitsu-kosan-builds-plant-for-lithium-sulphide/))
- **Solid electrolyte pilot plant (Chiba):** construction began in early 2026; several hundred t per year; completion scheduled for end of 2027, with operation in early 2028 ([Battery-News](https://battery-news.de/en/2026/02/03/idemitsu-starts-construction-of-pilot-plant-for-solid-electrolytes/))
- **Partners named for the Chiba pilot plant:** Toyota, Chiyoda Corporation and Sumitomo Metal ([Battery-Tech Network](https://battery-tech.net/battery-markets-news/idemitsu-kosan-opens-new-solid-electrolyte-pilot-plant/))
- **Intended customer:** Toyota; Idemitsu says the plant's materials are intended for Toyota's all-solid-state batteries
- **Commercialization target:** 2027 or 2028, aligned with Toyota's all-solid-state EV launch

## What It Is / How It Works

Sulfide solid electrolytes are the leading candidate for high-performance all-solid-state batteries because their ionic conductivity approaches that of liquid electrolytes. They are chemically sensitive, reacting with moisture and oxygen, which makes large-scale manufacturing hard.

Idemitsu is a materials producer, not a cell maker. Lithium sulfide is the key precursor from which sulfide electrolytes such as argyrodite-type compounds are synthesized, and Idemitsu makes it from sulfur recovered in petroleum refining (see [Sulfur]({{< relref "../resources/sulfur.md" >}})). In the Toyota cooperation, Idemitsu manufactures and mass-produces the solid electrolyte while Toyota develops the batteries and vehicles. The work runs in three phases: joint development and preparation of a large pilot facility, mass production at that facility, then study of full-scale commercialization ([Toyota](https://global.toyota/en/newsroom/corporate/39865919.html)).

The Chiba solid electrolyte pilot plant is the step after the lithium sulfide plant: it converts Li₂S into finished electrolyte in a controlled, large-format process. Mass-production process development is supported by Japan's NEDO agency under a green innovation program ([Battery-News](https://battery-news.de/en/2026/02/03/idemitsu-starts-construction-of-pilot-plant-for-solid-electrolytes/)).

## Supply Chain Position

Idemitsu operates at the **Precursor Chemicals → Electrolyte/Active Materials** layers as a raw material and electrolyte producer, not a cell maker. It is vertically integrated from petroleum refining (which produces [sulfur]({{< relref "../resources/sulfur.md" >}}) as a by-product) through [lithium sulfide]({{< relref "../resources/sulfur.md" >}}) to finished sulfide electrolyte; lithium inputs come via [lithium]({{< relref "../resources/lithium.md" >}}) commodity markets. The downstream customer named in sources is Toyota; no supply relationship with other sulfide developers such as [Solid Power]({{< relref "solid-power.md" >}}) or [QuantumScape]({{< relref "quantumscape.md" >}}) is disclosed in the sources reviewed.

**⚑ Key bottleneck:** the Chiba lithium sulfide plant (about 1,000 t per year, about 3 GWh equivalent) is the only large dedicated Li₂S investment documented in this knowledge base and its first intended customer is Toyota. Additional Li₂S producers will be needed as sulfide programs scale. Sulfur price volatility since 2025 (see Sulfur) is a cost risk for any producer outside a refinery-integrated position.
