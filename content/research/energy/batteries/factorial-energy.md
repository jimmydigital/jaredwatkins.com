## Summary

Factorial Energy is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based solid-state battery developer with two platforms: **FEST®**, a semi-solid hybrid-electrolyte platform whose 77 Ah cells Stellantis verified in lab testing at 375 Wh/kg, and **Solstice™**, an all-solid-state platform developed with Mercedes-Benz using a dry cathode process. It began trading on Nasdaq (FAC) on 8 June 2026 after a merger with the SPAC Cartesian Growth Corp III, and reported its first quarterly results as a public company on 11 August 2026. Named backers and partners include IQT, Mercedes-Benz, Stellantis, Hyundai and Kia; it is also pursuing drones, aerospace and robotics.

## What It Is / How It Works

FEST® is a semi-solid electrolyte, sometimes called a gel or hybrid electrolyte, positioned between conventional liquid electrolytes and fully solid ceramics. It targets the core trade-off in the solid-state space: fully solid electrolytes offer the best safety profile but are hard to manufacture and show high interface resistance, while liquid electrolytes process easily but are flammable and limit the cell to graphite anodes.

Factorial uses FEST® to enable a lithium metal anode (roughly ten times the theoretical capacity of graphite) while staying processable on conventional cell-making lines. The company says up to 80% of existing lithium-ion equipment can be reused, which would lower capital cost and learning curve for OEM partners; this has not been audited independently.

**Solstice™** is a fully all-solid-state design with a dry cathode process that avoids hazardous solvents and energy-intensive drying steps. The February 2026 MOU with Philenergy covers laser notching, precision stacking, winding and modular factory design for Solstice™ scale-up; financial terms, timeline and whether the MOU is binding were not disclosed.

In March 2026 Factorial announced strategic investments from IQT, which supports expansion into drones, UAVs and mobile robotics, and from POSCO Future M, named as a battery supply chain partner alongside Philenergy ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260310039385/en/Factorial-Drives-Solid-State-Battery-Expansion-to-Drones-and-Robotics-with-IQT-and-Strategic-Partners)). Karma Automotive and Factorial announced a joint programme in February 2026 to integrate FEST® cells into Karma's Kaveya, planned for late 2027, with the aim of validating the cells in a production passenger vehicle environment ([Electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/02/16/karma-automotive-validates-solid-state-battery-cells-from-factorial/)).

Factorial operates at the **Cell Manufacturing** layer.
