## Summary

Solid Power (NASDAQ: SLDP) is a Louisville, Colorado developer of sulfide-based solid electrolytes that sells electrolyte material and licenses cell designs rather than building finished cells at scale. Its electrolyte is evaluated with Samsung SDI and BMW under a Joint Evaluation Agreement, BMW has run a Solid Power-cell i7 test vehicle, and an SK On line installation agreement is complete. A continuous electrolyte pilot line is being installed, with acceptance testing targeted for Q3 2026 and startup in Q4 2026. The company reported total liquidity of $419.3 million at 30 June 2026 and no debt.

## What It Is / How It Works

Solid Power's model differs from most solid-state companies. Instead of becoming a vertically integrated cell maker, it develops a sulfide solid electrolyte as its core IP, supplies it to established cell manufacturers, and licenses cell design know-how. Samsung SDI would build cells, BMW would validate them in vehicles, and SK On is a second manufacturing relationship.

That approach avoids the capital cost of gigafactories but ties commercialization to its partners' integration and production timelines, a dependency that vertically integrated competitors such as [QuantumScape]({{< relref "quantumscape.md" >}}) or [Factorial Energy]({{< relref "factorial-energy.md" >}}) do not share. Sulfide electrolytes also depend on a high-purity lithium sulfide supply; see [Sulfur]({{< relref "../resources/sulfur.md" >}}).

## Supply Chain Position

Solid Power operates at the **Electrolyte / Active Materials** layer: it makes sulfide solid electrolyte and licenses the technology. Upstream, the chemistry requires lithium sulfide (Li₂S) as a precursor, and Solid Power has not disclosed its supplier; see [Sulfur]({{< relref "../resources/sulfur.md" >}}). Lithium metal for the anode links to [Lithium]({{< relref "../resources/lithium.md" >}}). Downstream, Samsung SDI and SK On are the cell manufacturing relationships and BMW is the vehicle validation partner.

**⚑ Shared supplier risk:** Li₂S supply is the same upstream bottleneck faced by other sulfide programs; [Idemitsu Kosan]({{< relref "idemitsu-solid-state.md" >}})'s planned plant is sized at about 1,000 t per year with Toyota as its first intended customer.
