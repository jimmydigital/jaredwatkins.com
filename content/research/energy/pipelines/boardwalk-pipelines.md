## Summary

Boardwalk Pipeline Partners, LP is an interstate natural gas pipeline operator wholly owned (through Boardwalk Pipelines Holding Corp.) by Loews Corporation as of 2026-06-30. Its June 2026 10-Q reports capex of $343.5M for the first half of 2026 (vs $121.9M in 2025), construction work in progress of $543.6M, and estimated revenues of $9.4B under executed precedent or long-term firm agreements for growth projects, subject to regulatory approvals and construction risk. End-use customers include electric power generators, local distribution companies, industrial and petrochemical users and LNG exporters; **the 10-Q excerpt reviewed named no data center customer** ([SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001336047/000133604726000011/bwp-20260630.htm)). Other project-level detail was not in the excerpt read and is not asserted here.

## Key Facts

- **Status:** active
- **Type:** Private subsidiary of public Loews (NYSE: L); value-chain position: **pipeline operator** (interstate)
- **Acquisition:** bought Spire Marketing LLC for $211.9M cash on 2026-04-30 (renamed Boardwalk Continuum Marketing)
- **Marcellus-to-Louisiana:** Texas Gas launched an open season in April 2025 for up to 2 Bcf/d of firm transportation moving Marcellus and Utica gas to markets from Ohio to Louisiana, mainly using existing infrastructure; the existing Texas Gas system is 5,975 miles ([PGJ](https://pgjonline.com/news/2025/april/boardwalk-s-texas-gas-launches-open-season-for-2-bcfd-marcellus-to-louisiana-pipeline-expansion))

## What It Is / How It Works

Boardwalk's Texas Gas and Gulf South systems link Appalachian and Gulf Coast supply with Louisiana and Southeast markets. Data center linkage, where it exists, is via gas deliveries to power generators; no BTM project was identified.
