## Overview

Gas-fired generation, grid-connected or behind-the-meter (BTM), needs firm gas delivery, and that is what pipeline operators sell. This section separates **operators** (own and run pipelines: [Kinder Morgan]({{< relref "kinder-morgan.md" >}}), [Energy Transfer]({{< relref "energy-transfer.md" >}}), [ONEOK]({{< relref "oneok.md" >}}), [Kinetik]({{< relref "kinetik.md" >}}), [Boardwalk]({{< relref "boardwalk-pipelines.md" >}}), and [Williams]({{< relref "../../datacenters/behind-meter-power/williams-companies-btm.md" >}}), covered in the Behind-the-Meter Power section) from **builders** ([MasTec]({{< relref "mastec.md" >}}), [Quanta]({{< relref "quanta-services.md" >}})) and from **input suppliers** in [Pipeline Components & Input Suppliers]({{< relref "components/_index.md" >}}). Generation equipment vendors are in [Behind-the-Meter Power]({{< relref "../../datacenters/behind-meter-power/_index.md" >}}).

"KMP" is Kinder Morgan Energy Partners, merged into Kinder Morgan, Inc. (KMI) in 2014; the entry is [Kinder Morgan]({{< relref "kinder-morgan.md" >}}).

## Key Themes

- **Most disclosed "data center" pipeline work serves utilities and grid-connected plants, not BTM campuses.** BTM-style disclosures: Energy Transfer's CloudBurst (up to 450,000 MMBtu/d, up to about 1.2 GW BTM, subject to FID) and Nexus campus, and Williams' projects. Kinder Morgan's $9.6B backlog (Q2 2026) is more than 60% power generation and distribution, with the data center share unquantified.
- **Contract quality varies.** Energy Transfer's own slide mixes signed agreements, a letter of intent and counterparty-conditional deals; ONEOK's 1 GW is undisclosed as to counterparty and appears only in call commentary, not its earnings release.
- **Demand is contested.** In October 2026 the Southern Environmental Law Center sued over FERC's approval of Kinder Morgan's MSX and SSE4, citing a London Economics International report that the pipelines would carry over four times utilities' forecast volumes, largely on speculative data centers. FERC found no basis to look behind contracts.
- **Builders and suppliers are the capacity read-through.** MasTec's pipeline backlog rose 35% sequentially to about $1.8B in Q2 2026; line pipe orders (Borusan Berg Pipe for Eiger Express; Welspun's unnamed U.S. orders) lead construction.
- **People links:** Kinetik's CEO and chief accounting officer are Energy Transfer alumni; ONEOK's Mark Roles is ex-Williams; Kinder Morgan's chairman, president and tax chief share an Enron background. See each entry's Key People.
- **Constraints:** turbine and compression lead times; Waha takeaway limits in the Permian.
