## Summary

ONEOK, Inc. (NYSE: OKE; headquartered in Tulsa, Oklahoma per its earnings release) is a midstream company centered on natural gas liquids, gas gathering and processing, and refined products and crude. It is a **secondary player in data center gas supply**: its Q2 2026 call reportedly disclosed a gas supply agreement for 1 GW of power-plant demand and late-stage talks on further data center supply that could each require more than $100M of capital with firm-demand contracts ([MarketBeat summary of the 2026-08-05 call](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/oneok-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-08-05/)). The customer, location and whether the plant is grid-connected or behind-the-meter were not stated in the source reviewed. The call figures are from a third-party summary; the earnings release is linked below but was not read in full.

## Key Facts

- **Status:** active
- **Type:** Public (NYSE: OKE); value-chain position: **pipeline operator / gatherer-processor**
- **Q2 2026:** net income $967M ($1.53/share, +13%); adjusted EBITDA $2.121B (+7%) per the earnings release (the MarketBeat call summary shows $965M). The earnings release does not mention power demand or data centers; the 1 GW and data center statements come from the call summary only
- **2026 guidance (midpoints, raised):** net income $3.6B; adjusted EBITDA $8.35B; capex $2.7B–$3.2B, likely near the high end
- **Power / data center:** gas supply agreement for 1 GW of power-plant demand; more data center opportunities in late-stage talks (each over $100M capex; firm-demand contracts)
- **Other projects:** Delaware Basin plant expansions (+110 MMcf/d, Q3 2026); Bighorn Plant to 400 MMcf/d (mid-2027); Cutter 2 (120 MMcf/d, Q1 2028); Medford fractionation phases (Q4 2026, Q1 2027); Denver refined-products expansion in service 2026-08-01

## What It Is / How It Works

Most ONEOK capital goes to gathering, processing, NGL transport and fractionation rather than long-haul gas transmission. Its data center exposure is via gas supply and delivery to power plants. Because disclosure is thin, treat the 1 GW figure as a demand-side description (plant capacity served), not a volume or revenue number.

## Notable Developments

- **Eiger Express:** ONEOK holds 15% directly and 25.5% in total, including its Matterhorn JV stake, of the roughly 450-mile, 42-inch, up to 2.5 Bcf/d Permian-to-Katy pipeline built and operated by WhiteWater, targeted for mid-2028 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/eiger-express-pipeline-boost-permian-145848600.html)); its pipe is supplied by [Borusan Berg Pipe]({{< relref "components/borusan-berg-pipe.md" >}}).
