## Summary

Kinetik Holdings Inc. (NYSE: KNTK) is a Permian Basin gas processor and pipeline owner. Its relevance here is two-fold: it moves and processes Delaware Basin gas that feeds Texas power and LNG demand, and it is itself building a small **behind-the-meter** power project to serve its own plant. Its Q2 2026 release cites "LNG exports and data center developments" as demand drivers for Permian gas but, in the portion reviewed, names no data center customer ([Business Wire, 2026-08-05](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260805695706/en/Kinetik-Reports-Record-Second-Quarter-2026-Results-and-Raises-Full-Year-2026-Guidance)).

## Key Facts

- **Status:** active
- **Type:** Public (NYSE: KNTK); value-chain position: **pipeline operator / gatherer-processor**
- **Q2 2026:** net income incl. noncontrolling interest $123.1M; adjusted EBITDA $280.8M; net debt $3.94B (3.85x)
- **2026 guidance (raised):** adjusted EBITDA $1.04B–$1.10B; capex about $560M
- **Kings Landing II:** FID May 2026; about $260M; mid-2028; system processing capacity 2.7 Bcf/d in 2028
- **Diamond Volt:** 40 MW behind-the-meter generation at the Diamond Cryo Complex; in service expected Q2 2027
- **Waha constraint:** about 250 MMcf/d of Waha-related shut-ins in Q2; about 25 MMcf/d average curtailments assumed in H2 2026
- **Pipelines:** Permian Highway and Shin Oak outperformed; ECCC pipeline placed in service, expansion anticipated 2027

## What It Is / How It Works

Kinetik gathers and processes gas in the Delaware Basin and transports residue gas and NGLs. Waha-hub price weakness and takeaway limits are the context for new Permian pipelines such as Hugh Brinson (see [Energy Transfer]({{< relref "energy-transfer.md" >}})) and Permian Link (see [Kinder Morgan]({{< relref "kinder-morgan.md" >}})). Diamond Volt is on-site power for Kinetik's own facility, not a data center supply project.
