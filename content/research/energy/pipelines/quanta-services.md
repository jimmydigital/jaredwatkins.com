## Summary

Quanta Services (NYSE: PWR) is a **builder** of electric transmission, power generation, and pipeline and industrial infrastructure. Its Q2 2026 call summary does not discuss gas pipeline work, so its role in this section is as the electrical and generation-side counterpart to [MasTec]({{< relref "mastec.md" >}}), including direct work with hyperscalers and selective behind-the-meter generation projects ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/quanta-services-q2-earnings-call-highlights)).

## Key Facts

- **Status:** active
- **Type:** Public (NYSE: PWR); value-chain position: **builder / contractor**
- **Q2 2026:** revenue $9.6B; net income attributable $451M ($2.96/share); adjusted EPS $4.24; adjusted EBITDA $1.1B; backlog record $53B
- **2026 guidance (raised):** revenue $39.3B–$39.7B; adjusted EBITDA $4.1B–$4.2B; adjusted EPS $16.45–$16.95; free cash flow $2B–$2.5B
- **Acquisitions:** Phalcon, Enerfab, Percheron, PSD; about $1.24B upfront plus up to $242M contingent
- **Data centers:** technology-related activity about 15-20% of the business; backlog includes data center work with limited notices to proceed
- **Generation:** selective on combined-cycle and some single-cycle; pursues BTM and front-of-the-meter projects when risk allocation is acceptable

## What It Is / How It Works

Quanta's data center exposure is mostly electrical (interconnection, transmission, on-site power). Backlog of $53B is not all data-center-related, and part of data center backlog is not yet released to proceed.
