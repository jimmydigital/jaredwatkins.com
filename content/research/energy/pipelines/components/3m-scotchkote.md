## Summary

3M™ Scotchkote™ Fusion-Bonded Epoxy Coating 6352 is a mechanically strong FBE **topcoat** bonded to a base coating, intended to protect the primary corrosion coating during directional drilling, bores, river crossings and rough-terrain installation; it meets AWWA C213 and sits under 3M's Safety & Industrial Business Group, Coatings > Corrosion Protection Coatings > Pipe Coatings ([3M](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/p/d/b5005048181/)). **Only this product page was reviewed:** no 3M segment results, pricing, plant locations or customer or project ties are asserted.

## What It Is / How It Works

Dual-layer FBE adds an abrasion-resistant outer layer for trenchless installation, which matters for river and road crossings on new gas lines.
