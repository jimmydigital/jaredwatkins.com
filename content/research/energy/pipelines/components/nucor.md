## Summary

Nucor Corporation (NYSE: NUE) is a steel producer, the raw-material layer beneath welded line pipe makers such as [Welspun]({{< relref "welspun-corp.md" >}}) and [Borusan Berg Pipe]({{< relref "borusan-berg-pipe.md" >}}). On its Q2 2026 call, the Brandenburg plate facility shipped over 230,000 tons, with nearly a third in grades and sizes it previously couldn't offer, **including API line pipe grades** ([Nasdaq call summary](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/nucor-q2-earnings-call-highlights)). Management cites energy infrastructure and data centers among demand drivers but the source does not discuss pipeline demand specifically, and **no pipe mill or pipeline project was identified as a Nucor customer** in the sources reviewed.

## What It Is / How It Works

Large-diameter welded pipe is rolled from plate or coil. Plate availability, price and domestic content rules therefore shape pipe lead times and cost. Plate mill commentary on API grades and order books is an upstream signal; a tie to a specific pipeline needs a mill, supplier or owner disclosure naming the steel source.
