## Summary

Welspun Corp is a Mumbai-based pipe maker whose U.S. subsidiaries are Welspun Tubular LLC (welded line pipe manufacturing and coating, Little Rock, Arkansas) and Welspun Pipes Inc. (Houston office) ([Steel Market Update](https://www.steelmarketupdate.com/2026/08/27/welspun-wins-1-8b-line-pipe-order/)). Four orders are reported, **none with a named customer or project**: about ₹2,400 crore of coated pipe for U.S. natural gas pipeline projects (March 2025, [EPC World](https://www.epcworld.in/welspun-corp-to-supply-coated-pipes-for-natural-gas-pipeline-projects-in-the-usa/)); a $1.8B line pipe order from its U.S. factory (2026-08-27, [SMU](https://www.steelmarketupdate.com/2026/08/27/welspun-wins-1-8b-line-pipe-order/)); a ₹960 crore coated line pipe order supplied from Little Rock, to be executed in FY27 and FY28, announced around 2026-07-27, lifting the order book to about ₹25,750 crore, a record ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/stocks/welspun-corp-share-price-gains-after-960-crore-us-order-win-q1-fy27-profit-jumps-200)); and a $413M HFIW order from an unnamed international client, to be executed in FY2027-28 to FY2028-29, from the upgraded Little Rock HFIW mill (2026-09-25, [SteelOrbis](https://www.steelorbis.com/steel-news/latest-news/indias-welspun-corps-us-subsidiary-receives-413-million-order-for-hfiw-pipe-supply-1479761.htm)). The $413M order is reported as for an international client, so it is not a U.S. pipeline signal.
