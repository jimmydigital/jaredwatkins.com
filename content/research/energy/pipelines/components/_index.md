## Overview

Pipeline owners and builders are the visible layer; the inputs are bought earlier. Line pipe is ordered months to years before construction, so mill order announcements, plant utilization commentary, and supplier quarterly reports can move before an operator's backlog or a contractor's revenue. This subsection tracks input suppliers and tries to tie their orders to named projects. Operators are in [Pipelines]({{< relref "../_index.md" >}}); the contractor view is [MasTec]({{< relref "../mastec.md" >}}) and [Quanta]({{< relref "../quanta-services.md" >}}).

## Key Themes

- **Only one verified project tie:** Borusan Berg Pipe's $567M pipe supply for Eiger Express (ONEOK 25.5% total interest; WhiteWater builds and operates; mid-2028 target).
- **Welspun's U.S. orders are unmatched:** $1.8B (2026-08-27), ₹960 crore (about 2026-07-27), ₹2,400 crore (2025-03) and a $413M HFIW order for an international client; none names a customer or project.
- **Cancellations show up in coater backlog:** L.B. Foster's Infrastructure backlog fell $34.5M year over year, about $19M from a cancelled Summit pipeline coating order.
- **FBE chain now mapped at vendor level:** [Axalta Nap-Gard]({{< relref "axalta-nap-gard.md" >}}) and [3M Scotchkote]({{< relref "3m-scotchkote.md" >}}) (powder), [Dura-Bond]({{< relref "dura-bond.md" >}}) and [L.B. - **Valves and compression:** [SLB]({{< relref "slb-valves.md" >}}) and [Flowserve]({{< relref "flowserve.md" >}}) (valves, energy bookings +48%), [Baker Hughes]({{< relref "baker-hughes.md" >}}) (compression, record backlog).

### Signals to Monitor

- Supplier order announcements and exchange filings (Borusan KAP, Welspun) matched to owner project timing and pipe diameter.
- Plant utilization and backlog commentary in supplier quarterly reports.
- Local news near plants (Mobile AL, Panama City FL, Little Rock AR) on hiring, shifts, rail and port activity.
- Contractor backlog ([MasTec]({{< relref "../mastec.md" >}}) pipeline backlog about $1.8B, Q2 2026) as the lagging confirmation.
- Trade actions: Steel Market Update headlines mention higher anti-dumping duties on large-diameter welded pipe from Turkey; the article was not read, so this is a lead only.
