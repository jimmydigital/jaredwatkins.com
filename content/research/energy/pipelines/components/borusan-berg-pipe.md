## Summary

Borusan Berg Pipe is the U.S. subsidiary of Turkish producer Borusan Boru. Per trade coverage it makes large-diameter steel pipe at facilities in Mobile, Alabama and Panama City, Florida ([Tube Tradefair](https://www.tube-tradefair.com/en/Media_News/News/Industry_News/Borusan_Berg_Pipe_Wins_$567_Million_Contract_for_Landmark_U.S._Pipeline_Project); [Pipeline Journal](https://pipeline-journal.net/news/turkish-pipe-producer-borusan-secures-567m-supply-deal-eiger-express-pipeline)). The only project tie verified here is **Eiger Express**: a $567M sales agreement with Eiger Supply LLC for about 107 cm (42-inch) pipe, deliveries scheduled throughout 2026. Eiger Express is a roughly 450-mile, 42-inch, up to 2.5 Bcf/d line from the Permian to Katy, Texas, owned through the Matterhorn JV (WhiteWater, MPLX, Enbridge; 70%) with [ONEOK]({{< relref "../oneok.md" >}}) and MPLX 15% each; WhiteWater builds and operates it ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/eiger-express-pipeline-boost-permian-145848600.html)). One source describes the pipe as covering about 725 km, consistent with that length.

## What It Is / How It Works

Large-diameter welded line pipe is bought by the pipeline owner (or its builder) well ahead of construction. A mill order is therefore one of the earliest observable signals that a project is moving from permit to build.
