## Summary

Flowserve Corporation (NYSE: FLS; headquartered in Irving, Texas per its 10-Q cover) makes pumps, valves and seals. In Q2 2026 sales were $1.2B (-2%), bookings rose 26% to $1.35B (book-to-bill 1.15), and **energy bookings rose 48%**, helped by large LNG awards in the Middle East and Canada; management cited longer-term opportunities in energy security including redundant pipelines and storage, without sizing them ([Nasdaq call summary](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/flowserve-q2-earnings-call-highlights)). The valve businesses described in the 10-Q excerpt (MOGAS severe-service valves; Trillium Flow Technologies' valves for power generation) are not mainline gas transmission valves, and **no pipeline project order was found** ([SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000030625/000003062526000020/fls-20260630.htm)).
