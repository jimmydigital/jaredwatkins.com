## Summary

Axalta Coating Systems (NYSE: AXTA; Philadelphia dateline) sells **Nap-Gard**, which it describes as fusion-bonded epoxy powder coatings used on the inside and outside of pipe, valves, girth welds and pipe repairs, with single-, dual- and multi-layer systems for buried and subsea pipelines ([Axalta](https://www.axalta.com/au/en_GB/products-and-customers/powder-coatings-/nap-gard-powder-coating.html)). This is the **FBE chemical layer** of the pipeline supply chain. Axalta's Q2 2026 results show net sales of $1.35B, Industrial sales of $327M (+2%) with lower North American volume, and no pipeline-specific commentary; the release's main pending item was a proposed merger of equals with AkzoNobel (special general meeting scheduled August 5; outcome not verified here) ([StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Press+Releases/Axalta+Releases+Second+Quarter+2026+Results/26821325.html)).

## What It Is / How It Works

FBE powder is applied by plant coaters (see [Dura-Bond]({{< relref "dura-bond.md" >}}) and [L.B. Foster]({{< relref "lb-foster.md" >}})) to heated pipe, where it melts and fuses to the steel. Powder demand therefore tracks coated-pipe production but is bundled in Axalta's broad Industrial results, so it is a weak standalone signal.
