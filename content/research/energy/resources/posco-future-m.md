## Summary

POSCO Future M is a South Korean battery materials company that produces both cathode and anode active materials, and a subsidiary of the POSCO Group. It supplies cell makers and automakers, runs the Ultium CAM cathode joint venture with GM in Quebec, signed a non-binding MOU with Factorial Energy in December 2025 on all-solid-state battery materials, and reported a return to a small operating profit in Q2 2026.

## Key Facts

- **Type:** Public subsidiary of the POSCO Group (KRX: 003670); POSCO Holdings ([NYSE: PKX](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/PKX)) holds 59.7% ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POSCO_Future_M))
- **HQ:** Pohang, South Korea ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POSCO_Future_M))
- **Products:** cathode active materials (NMC grades including high-nickel, and others) and anode active materials (graphite, silicon-based; lithium-metal anode materials are researched at POSCO Group level)
- **Q2 2026 (preliminary, announced 30 July):** revenue KRW 679.5 billion (up 2.8%), operating profit KRW 26.7 billion (up 3,351%), net profit KRW 24.7 billion ([SMM](https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/104036770-posco-future-m-announces-q2-earnings)); POSCO Holdings attributes the improvement to cathode inventory valuation gains and a recovery in anode sales volume to major customers ([POSCO Newsroom](https://newsroom.posco.com/en/posco-holdings-announces-q2-2026-results-profitability-improved-on-visible-gains-centered-on-lithium-and-lng-resources/))
- **Ultium CAM (Bécancour, Quebec):** POSCO Future M 85% and GM 15%; phase one planned for late October 2026 at about 20,000 t per year of high-nickel cathode, all supplied to Ultium Cells; originally targeted for September 2023 and postponed after EV demand slowed ([Advanced Carbons Council](https://advancedcarbonscouncil.org/blogpost/2151389/514558/Posco-Future-M-to-begin-Quebec-cathode-plant-operations-in-October-2026))

## What It Is / How It Works

POSCO Future M sits mid-stream in the battery supply chain: it processes nickel, cobalt, manganese, lithium and graphite into the active electrode powders that cell manufacturers coat onto current collectors. Handling both cathode and anode materials is unusual, since most materials companies specialize in one.

Under the Factorial MOU, POSCO Future M is researching cathode and silicon anode materials for all-solid-state cells, while the wider POSCO Group works on lithium-metal anode materials and sulfide-based solid electrolytes ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251203158606/en/POSCO-Future-M-Signs-MOU-with-U.S.-based-Factorial-for-All-Solid-State-Battery-Technology-Development)). Solid-state cells place different demands on materials than liquid-electrolyte cells: the anode is lithium metal or pre-lithiated rather than graphite, and the cathode-electrolyte interface must work with solid contacts. Commercial terms are expected only in future definitive agreements.

## Supply Chain Position

POSCO Future M sits at the **Precursor Chemicals → Electrolyte/Active Materials** layer, in cathode and anode active material manufacturing. Upstream it buys nickel, cobalt, manganese and lithium chemicals and graphite on the open market and through POSCO Group; see [Nickel]({{< relref "nickel.md" >}}), [Cobalt]({{< relref "cobalt.md" >}}) and [Lithium]({{< relref "lithium.md" >}}) for the concentration risks at each. Downstream, the Ultium CAM plant supplies Ultium Cells, and POSCO Future M's other documented customers are US automakers (anode deal) and Factorial (MOU).
