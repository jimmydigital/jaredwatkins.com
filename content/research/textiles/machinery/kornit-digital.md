## Summary

Kornit Digital builds industrial inkjet printers for garments and fabric using its own pigment inks, and a print-on-demand platform (KornitX). It is the leading digital-first printing vendor found in this review, though its systems target apparel and custom decoration more than long-run quilting prints.

## Key Facts

- Type: company (machine and consumables builder); public
- Status: Active
- Listing: Nasdaq, ticker KRNT (IPO 2015)
- HQ: Rosh HaAyin, Israel; Americas operations in Englewood, NJ
- Founded: 2002 per Wikipedia (founder Ofer Ben Zur); company site timeline says 2004 — sources conflict

- Employees: 700+ (Wikipedia, 2025); 650+ (company site)

- Acquisitions: Custom Gateway (UK, 2020), Voxel8 (Massachusetts, 2021), Tesoma (Germany, 2022), PrintFactory (Netherlands, 2026)

## What It Is / How It Works

Products include Avalanche Poly Pro (polyester), Presto and Presto MAX (direct-to-fabric), Apollo, and Atlas MAX Plus / Atlas MATRIX (industrial). Technology includes NeoPigment ink (patented 2015), a PreT system that applies fixation agent automatically, and ink production at Kiryat Gat. The company markets its systems as an alternative to screen printing, dye sublimation, and reactive dyes. The company reports a "1300+" customer base and shipped its 1,000th system in 2013.
