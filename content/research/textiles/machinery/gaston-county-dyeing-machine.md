## Summary

Gaston County Dyeing Machine Company (Stanley, North Carolina) designs and builds wet-processing machines and controls. It is one of the few U.S.-based dyeing-equipment builders found, and relevant to cotton fabric because dyeing and finishing determine color consistency and fastness.

- Installed base: more than 80 countries (company claim)
- Products: jet piece dyeing machines; cloth beam dyeing machines; yarn package/beam dyeing and drying systems; control and dyehouse management systems; sample/laboratory machines; Millennium Dyeing System (automated weighing, dispensing, heating, dyeing, washing); parts

## What It Is / How It Works

Burlington assigned rights to Victor Fahringer's jet-dyeing invention (US Patent 2,978,291) to the company, which introduced the first production jet dyeing machine at the 1961 AATCC convention in Atlantic City; the machine transformed knit dyeing. The company later commercialized the FFT (Foam Finishing Technology) low-energy finishing process, developed by Union Carbide's George M. Bryant and Andrew T. Walter (patent issued 1985). Cloth beam machines suit fabrics that rope-mark or lose width in rope form. ITMF 2023 data show jigger/beam dyeing shipments of 715 units worldwide (see [Textile Machinery Makers]({{< relref "textile-machinery-makers.md" >}})).

## Notable Developments
