## Summary

Rieter is a Swiss group that builds machinery and components for converting fiber into yarn: blowroom, cards, combers, ring, compact, rotor, and air-jet spinning machines, and winding. It describes itself as the world's leading supplier of staple-fiber spinning systems.

- Type: company (machine builder); public

## What It Is / How It Works

Founded in 1795 as a trading company, Rieter entered spinning in 1812 and became a machinery maker after 1830. It separated its automotive division as Autoneum in 2011. Acquisitions broadened its range: SSM Textile Machinery Division (30 June 2017), three Saurer businesses including Schlafhorst winders (agreement 13 August 2021), and Barmag from OC Oerlikon (2 February 2026). Production sites include Switzerland, China (Zhongshan), Germany, and Italy; the 2024 annual report cites production in ten countries. ITMF data show 85% of 2023 short-staple spindle shipments went to Asia & Oceania (see [Textile Machinery Makers]({{< relref "textile-machinery-makers.md" >}})).
