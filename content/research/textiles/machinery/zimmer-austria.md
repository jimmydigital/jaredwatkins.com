## Summary

Zimmer Austria builds both traditional and digital fabric printing systems. It supplied the 97:3 traditional-to-digital printing split cited in this section, and its COLARIS pigment printers are an example of digital moving toward cotton home textiles.

## Key Facts

- Type: company (machine builder); owner not stated
- Status: Active
- Legal entity: J. Zimmer Maschinenbau Gesellschaft mbH
- Sites: Kufstein, Austria (digital printing systems); Klagenfurt, Austria (screen printing, coating, heat treatment)
- Value-chain position: Machine builder (printing, coating, drying)
- Scale: 5,100+ machines installed; 2,690+ customers; "150+ years" (founding year not given) — company claims
- Products: ROTASCREEN TG/TU rotary screen machines; MAGNOPRINT flatscreen; COLARIS and CHROMOJET digital systems; coating lines; MODUS dryers and loop steamers
- COLARIS pigment printers: seven models, print widths 74–3,400 mm, 140–1,060 m²/h, Fujifilm Dimatix StarFire printheads

## What It Is / How It Works

Zimmer's own market data, reported in 2021, show a 97:3 traditional-to-digital product mix, pigments at 45% and reactive dyes at 27% of traditional printing, and in digital printing sublimation 57% and reactive 36%. Pigment printing needs little wastewater and suits home textiles, bed linen, upholstery, and outdoor fabric. Cost trade-offs include avoided screen engraving and storage versus digital ink and maintenance costs.
