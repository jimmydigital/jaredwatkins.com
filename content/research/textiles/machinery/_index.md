## Overview

Fabric production depends on a short list of machine families: gins, spinning frames, weaving looms, dyeing and finishing ranges, and printing machines. This subsection maps which companies build each and where they are based. See [Textile Machinery Makers]({{< relref "textile-machinery-makers.md" >}}).

## Key Themes

- **Machine building is European and Japanese, with Chinese and Indian entrants.** The leading loom and spinning-system makers named in sources are headquartered in Belgium, Italy, Switzerland, Germany, and Japan.
- **U.S. strength is narrow.** Cotton ginning (Lummus) and some wet-processing equipment (Gaston County Dyeing Machine) are documented U.S.-based; no U.S.-headquartered maker of shuttleless looms or spinning frames surfaced in this review.
- **Offshoring of machine building has precedent.** Continental Eagle announced in 2009 that it would move main gin-machinery production from Prattville, Alabama, to India, and ended U.S. production in 2012.
- **Demand is in Asia.** Asia & Oceania took 85% of short-staple spindles and 96% of shuttleless looms shipped in 2023.
