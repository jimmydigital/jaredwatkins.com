## Overview

Quilting fabric is the first deep-dive in the Textiles research area: plain-weave 100% cotton cloth sold in solids and prints to roughly 9–11 million U.S. quilters. It is a useful test case for reindustrialization because it combines commodity inputs (cotton, dyes), mature machinery, and a consumer market that is large, loyal, and almost entirely supplied from Asia.

## Key Themes

- **Quality is multi-factor and mostly unmeasured publicly.** Thread count, weight consistency, finishing, dye fastness, and print registration all matter; none is published consistently.
- **Brands do not name mills.** The only origin survey found is from 2017 and points to Korea, Japan, Indonesia, Thailand, and India, with one U.S. line.
- **The U.S. grows the cotton but ships it away.** About 90% of projected 2026/27 U.S. production is exported raw.
- **A domestic chain is forming outside quilting.** Weaving, digital printing, finishing, and sewing partners in the Carolinas could in principle serve quilting cloth.
