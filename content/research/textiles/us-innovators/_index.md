## Overview

This subsection tracks U.S. companies that make, finish, or reinvent textiles, with priority on startups and domestic capacity-builders over incumbents. None of the entries here is yet a quilting-fabric producer at scale; the nearest are a U.S. hand-dyed cotton studio and a vertically integrated domestic woven-fabric chain. See [Textile Innovation Overview]({{< relref "textile-innovation-overview.md" >}}) for the wider landscape.

## Key Themes

- **Downstream first.** Documented U.S. investment is concentrated in yarn, cut-and-sew, software, and finishing rather than in new cotton weaving capacity.
- **Defense and technical textiles lead.** Anatar and Sans Technical Fibers sell to defense and industrial customers; Berry Amendment compliance is a selling point.
- **Chains are re-forming regionally.** Hamrick Mills, Springs Digital, Tryon Finishing, and Carolina Creative Products announced a single-region woven-to-sewn chain in June 2026.
- **Evidence is thin.** Most company claims here are self-reported and unverified; each entry says so.
