## Summary

Anatar describes itself as a "materials innovation and advanced manufacturing company" that produces technical textiles and soft goods for aerospace and defense. Its published capabilities are product development, automated U.S. cut-and-sew, and traceability software; the site describes no fiber, weaving, or dyeing operation, so it is a manufacturing-as-a-service integrator, not a fabric mill, and is not a quilting-fabric company.

## Key Facts

- Type: company

## What It Is / How It Works

Anatar's operating model lists Materials Innovation, Manufacturing-as-a-Service, and Software. Capabilities pages describe four offerings: product development (tech packs, prototyping, material sourcing, patterning, grading); U.S. manufacturing (automated cut-and-sew, short-run to scale, Berry-compliant); "Loom" (supply chain visibility, a Digital Product Passport, consumer experience features; RFID, NFC, QR, and blockchain are named); and distribution and logistics. It trademarks "Seed to Sale" and "Concept to Consumer." Customers are not named; its sectors are aerospace and defense.
