## Summary

Cherrywood Fabrics hand-dyes 100% cotton in Minnesota and sells it as quilting, garment, and art-quilt fabric with a suede-like, tone-on-tone surface. It is a small artisan producer, not a mass-market mill, but it is the most direct U.S.-made, quilting-specific dyer identified.

## Key Facts

- Type: company
- Status: Active
- Location: studio at 7882 College Road, Baxter (Brainerd), MN 56425 (company site)
- Owner: Karla Overland (per Midwest Fiber Arts Trails; page undated)
- Value-chain position: Dyer / converter; base-cloth supplier not disclosed
- Products: yardage, bundles, patterns and kits, notions, color cards, annual art quilt challenge
- Dyeing: hand-dyed, with a privately designed color line; company cites about 25 years' experience (undated)

## What It Is / How It Works

The company says its 100% cotton cloth "is produced in Minnesota" and that its quality is well above mass-produced fabric. The dye chemistry, whether reactive dyes are used, and where the undyed cotton is woven are not disclosed in the sources reviewed.
