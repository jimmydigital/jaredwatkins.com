## Summary

Teijin built its first U.S. structural carbon fiber plant in Greenwood, South Carolina, on a site chosen for textile-trained labor and rail and port access. In December 2025 the company confirmed a temporary suspension starting mid-January 2026 with no restart date.

## Key Facts

- Type: company (subsidiary of Teijin Ltd., Japan)
- Status: Production suspended from mid-January 2026 (announced 2025-12-31); restart depends on global market recovery
- Site: Greenwood, SC (CompositesWorld says Greenville County; the Index-Journal and Textile Excellence say Greenwood). Plant 185,000 sq ft on about 12 acres of a roughly 440-acre site; land bought 2017; groundbreaking June 2018
- Investment: announced 2016 as $600M with 220 jobs; one 2017 article instead cites USD 320M (discrepancy not resolved)
- Products: 12K and 24K fibers, standard and intermediate modulus; high modulus in preliminary work
- Precursor: PAN imported from Teijin's Mishima plant (Shizuoka, Japan); a Greenwood precursor line "could be part of future expansion"
- Capacity: not disclosed; the line is reportedly similar to the 2,700 t/yr line Teijin added at Mishima in 2008
- Targets: aerospace, automotive, hydrogen storage (Type 4 pressure vessels)
- Earlier U.S. history: a Tenax line in Rockwood, TN, converted to Pyromex oxidized PAN fiber

## What It Is / How It Works

PAN precursor is mounted on creels and pulled through a series of oxidation ovens, then converted to carbon (see [Carbon Fiber Machinery]({{< relref "carbon-fiber-machinery.md" >}})). The plant includes exhaust filtration and water recycling with no wastewater released. Teijin also owns Continental Structural Plastics (acquired 2016 for US$825M per Textile Excellence).

## Notable Developments

- **2026-01 (mid):** Planned production pause; more than 95% of employees affected per the company president, about 80 per the county development director (difference unexplained).
- **2024-05:** CompositesWorld plant tour; lines had operated almost two years; standard-modulus fiber aerospace-grade a little over a year.
- **2018-06:** Groundbreaking.
