## Summary

Harper International builds the heat-treatment equipment at the center of a carbon fiber line: oxidation ovens and low- and high-temperature carbonization furnaces, plus energy recovery and exhaust cleaning.

## Key Facts

- Type: company (equipment supplier); ownership not checked
- HQ: Lancaster, N.Y. per 2011 product article; Buffalo, NY per 2018 article (different sources)
- Supplies: oxidation ovens, LT and HT carbonization furnaces, integrated energy recovery, process exhaust gas cleaning
- 2018 announcement (28 Feb): contracts worth approximately $90M with unnamed "prominent international firms"
- 2011 oven launch: tow band widths from 300 mm (11.8 in) to over 4,000 mm (157.5 in); modular design said to cut field installation labor by 90% on a 300-mm pilot line; proprietary atmospheric seals to reduce fugitive emissions

## What It Is / How It Works

The oxidation oven stabilizes precursor fiber; furnaces then carbonize it. Harper's claims centre on temperature and velocity uniformity and lower energy use. See [Carbon Fiber Machinery]({{< relref "carbon-fiber-machinery.md" >}}).
