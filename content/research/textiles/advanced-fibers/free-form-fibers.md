## Summary

Free Form Fibers (FFF) uses laser chemical vapor deposition (LCVD) to grow SiC fibers directly from gas, an alternative to the polymer-derived route used by established SiC fiber makers.

## Key Facts

- Type: startup; Saratoga Springs, NY
- Process: ~20 micron laser spot heats a substrate in precursor gas; fiber grown by pulling at 30–300 microns/second; one tool can run up to 1,200 parallel beams
- Product: LP30-SC; 71:29 Si:C described as best for 1,500°C; strength 3–6 GPa (7–8 GPa for 77:23)
- Creep claim: survived 175 hours at 1,500°C and 700 MPa, versus Hi-Nicalon Type S fracturing after roughly 75–150 hours (company testing)
- Length demonstrated: up to 32 ft strands; typical arrays of 50 fibers at 2 ft
- Funding: not stated in the article read (SBIR listings appeared in search but were not fetched)
