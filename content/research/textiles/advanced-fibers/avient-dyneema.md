## Summary

Dyneema, invented at DSM in the 1960s–80s, is now part of Avient. It is the main competitor to Solstice's Spectra and is sold into armor, ropes and industrial uses.

## Key Facts

- Type: company / brand; Avient is public
- Owner HQ: Avon Lake, OH; acquired Dyneema in 2022
- U.S. production: Greenville, NC; the site map also lists Mesa, AZ among five manufacturing plants (others: Heerlen NL, Laiwu CN, Tsuruga JP)
- History: DSM patented gel spinning in the 1970s and commercialized Dyneema in the 1980s
- Applications: aquaculture, maritime mooring, offshore energy, law enforcement and military, manufacturing and construction, sports
