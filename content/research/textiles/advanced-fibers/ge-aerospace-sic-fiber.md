## Summary

GE planned the first U.S. silicon carbide (SiC) ceramic fiber factory and a companion tape factory to supply ceramic matrix composite (CMC) engine parts made at its Asheville, NC facility.

## Key Facts

- Type: facility / project (GE Aerospace)
- Investment: more than $200M; two factories on 100 acres in Huntsville, AL; up to 300 jobs
- Factory 1: SiC fiber for the U.S. Department of Defense, GE, Herakles Safran and others subject to U.S. regulations; AFRL Title III funding
- Factory 2: GE-only CMC tape plant applying proprietary coatings
- Technology: licensed from NGS Advanced Fibers (Nippon Carbon, GE, Herakles Safran), the only large-scale SiC fiber plant at the time (Japan)
- End uses: jet engines, land-based turbines
- Timeline: announced Nov 2015; groundbreaking 16 June 2016; operations expected 2018
