## Summary

Kevlar is the best-known aramid fiber. DuPont built large U.S. plants for it; as of April 2026 the business belongs to Arclin, a TJC portfolio company.

## Key Facts

- Type: product line / business; ownership changed
- Owner: Arclin (Alpharetta, GA; TJC affiliate) since 1 April 2026; deal valued about $1.8B (about $1.2B pre-tax cash, $300M note, 16% equity stake for DuPont)
- Cooper River (Charleston, SC): $500M expansion described in 2011 as operational, the largest single Kevlar investment since 1965; initial grades Kevlar AP, KM2 Plus, XP
- Spruance (Richmond, VA): $50M expansion
- Capacity: 25% global capacity increase initially, 40% expected total (2011 article)

## What It Is / How It Works

Kevlar is used in body armor and other protection; see [Advanced Fiber Materials]({{< relref "advanced-fiber-materials.md" >}}) for comparison with UHMWPE.
