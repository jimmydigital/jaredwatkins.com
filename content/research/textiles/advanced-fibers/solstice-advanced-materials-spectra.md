## Summary

Solstice Advanced Materials, spun out of Honeywell in late 2025, owns the Spectra and Spectra Shield ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) fiber and composite business and is expanding its Virginia ballistic fiber plant.

## Key Facts

- Type: company; public (Nasdaq: SOLS per Honeywell's 2025 release; spin-off distribution expected 30 Oct 2025 at one Solstice share per four Honeywell shares)
- Site: Colonial Heights, Chesterfield County, VA (existing ballistic fiber plant)
- Investment: more than $220M across multiple projects; about 100 new jobs by 2029; construction 2025–2029, projected completion 30 Sept 2030
- New capability: fiber spinning, composite coating, analytical
- Product claims: up to 15 times stronger than steel and 65% lighter than aramid fabrics (company-sourced)
- Uses: helmets, vests, vehicle armor, naval and industrial applications
- Capacity figures: not given

## What It Is / How It Works

UHMWPE fiber is made by gel spinning; Spectra competes with [Avient Dyneema]({{< relref "avient-dyneema.md" >}}) and with aramids (see [Advanced Fiber Materials]({{< relref "advanced-fiber-materials.md" >}})).
