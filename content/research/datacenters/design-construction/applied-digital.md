## Summary

Applied Digital (NASDAQ: APLD) is a Dallas, Texas developer and operator of purpose-built AI datacenter campuses it calls "AI Factories," leased on roughly 15-year take-or-pay terms to CoreWeave and to an unnamed high investment-grade hyperscaler. Its fiscal year ended 2026-05-31 reported revenue of $611.3M and a GAAP net loss of $249.2M attributable to common stockholders. The company reports about 1,410 MW of contracted critical IT load across five campuses and about $36B of base-term contracted revenue (about $86B if all renewal options are exercised). Only part of that capacity is operating: Polaris Forge 1 in Ellendale, North Dakota had 175 MW live as of 2026-06-30, and the later campuses are under construction with operations expected in 2027–2028.

## What It Is / How It Works

Applied Digital builds powered shells with fit-out for a single anchor tenant and leases them on long terms. The tenant brings its own GPUs; Applied Digital supplies land, power connection, structure, cooling and connectivity. Applied Digital carries construction and financing risk; the tenant carries hardware and utilization risk. Contracted revenue is therefore lease revenue, not compute revenue.

**Site strategy.** Early campuses are in North Dakota, chosen for available power, a cool climate and land. Later campuses moved to Louisiana and another southern state to reach additional hyperscaler demand. Delta Forge 1 broke ground in January 2026 with initial operations expected in 2027.

**Cooling.** The company describes a closed-loop direct-to-chip liquid system with dry heat rejection, rather than evaporative cooling towers. Its releases cite a design PUE of 1.18 and near-zero water consumption for Polaris Forge 1 and 2. The fluid and architecture are not disclosed in detail.

**Tenant concentration.** CoreWeave is the tenant on the existing Polaris Forge 1 leases; the three newest leases are with the same unnamed hyperscaler, which Applied Digital describes as high investment-grade. Neither the identity nor the credit terms of that tenant are public.

## Supply Chain Position

Applied Digital sits at the **datacenter developer/operator** layer: downstream of land, utility power, construction and cooling equipment; upstream of tenants who supply GPUs.

| Layer | Applied Digital's dependency |
|-------|------------------------------|
| Land and site | North Dakota, Louisiana and one unnamed southern state |
| Utility interconnect | Utility power is the main constraint; the company cites about 2.15 GW of grid-connected utility power for its five campuses |
| Construction | General contractors not named in sources reviewed |
| Cooling | Proprietary closed-loop direct-to-chip system; component suppliers not disclosed |
| Tenants | CoreWeave (PF1); unnamed hyperscaler (PF2, PF3, DF1, DF2) |

**⚑ Tenant concentration:** Four of five campuses are leased to one unnamed hyperscaler and the first campus to CoreWeave. The FY2026 release says CoreWeave leases are supported by a restructured SPV, springing guarantees from CoreWeave, Inc. and a $50M letter of credit, and that a memorandum of understanding would assign the Polaris Forge 1 Building 4 lease to a CoreWeave subsidiary if it achieves an investment-grade rating.

**⚑ Financing:** Funding relies on high-yield secured notes (6.750% and 7.000%), a Macquarie preferred equity facility and construction bridge loans. About $5.0B of debt against about $4.2B of cash and restricted cash at 2026-05-31.
