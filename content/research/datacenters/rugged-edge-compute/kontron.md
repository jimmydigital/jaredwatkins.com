## Summary

Kontron AG (Xetra: KTN, also SDAX and TecDAX) is a Linz, Austria technology group whose Aerospace & Defense business sells VPX computing for military and security systems. It is much broader than rugged edge compute: other lines include cybersolutions, transportation and GreenTec. For H1 2026 it reported revenue of €737.1M, adjusted EBITDA of €100.8M, an order backlog of €2,750M and Aerospace & Defense growth of 31.8%. Full-year 2026 guidance was confirmed on 2026-08-06: revenue slightly above 2025's €1,607M and adjusted EBITDA of €225M before about €25M of restructuring. Its main defense signal in this section is a December 2024 order worth around €165M for VPX units for surveillance systems.

## What It Is / How It Works

Kontron sells VPX boards, subsystems and mission computers into European defense and security programs. It competes at the module and subsystem tier with [Mercury Systems]({{< relref "mercury-systems.md" >}}) and [Curtiss-Wright]({{< relref "curtiss-wright.md" >}}), but the sources reviewed describe no head-to-head bids. The 2024 order shows the type of work: VPX units supplied to a European sensor and systems company building surveillance systems.

Product-level details earlier attributed to Kontron's µDARC microserver and HARAKAN mission computer (weights, power, temperature range, SOSA alignment) were not confirmed from sources fetched in this review. The ITAR-free claim comes from Kontron marketing and was not re-checked.
